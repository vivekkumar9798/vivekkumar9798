### 👨‍💻 About me

I build fast, polished mobile apps for Android and iOS. I enjoy solving the hard problems: real-time video, background audio, camera and on-device machine learning, and taking apps all the way through Play Store review.

- 📱 **5+ years** of experience shipping Android, React Native and cross-platform apps
- 🧩 Native Android development with **Kotlin, Jetpack Compose** and a clean MVVM architecture
- 🌍 Shared codebases across platforms with **Kotlin Multiplatform** and **React Native**
- 🎥 Real-time audio and video communication with **Agora, Twilio and LiveKit**
- 🤖 On-device machine learning with **TensorFlow Lite and ML Kit**, plus 3D object scanning
- 🚀 End-to-end releases: app signing, App Bundles, Play Console and store listings
